# NEURODECK Brand Guidelines & Design Notes

## Visual DNA
NEURODECK is a premium handheld AI workstation optimized for the Steam Deck.
Its visual style is defined as **Tactical Glass** — combining console-grade dashboard clarity, subtle cyberpunk terminal cues, and AAA command center graphics.

---

---

## Typography
* **Main Title:** Orbitron or JetBrains Mono, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.16em` or more.
* **Taglines & Subtext:** Inter or JetBrains Mono, bold, uppercase, high letter-spacing.
* **Logs & Metrics:** Fira Code, JetBrains Mono, or Consolas for tabular alignment.

---

## Graphic Layout Rules
1. **The Circuit Monogram (Glyph):**
   - Must retain the 3-stroke angular `N` monogram.
   - Circular nodes (white centers with glowing cyan halos) must sit at the four corners.
   - Translucent horizontal tails must extend outwards to simulate integrated circuitry.
2. **Safe Areas for Handhelds:**
   - Keep logos and critical content within the central safe area (inset by at least 10% on all edges) to respect SteamOS HUD and layout cropping on 16:10 screens.
3. **No Cyber-Slop:**
   - Avoid generic AI brains, cryptocurrency or esports-styled badges, and raw circuit board textures. Keep illustrations abstract, geometric, and functional.
