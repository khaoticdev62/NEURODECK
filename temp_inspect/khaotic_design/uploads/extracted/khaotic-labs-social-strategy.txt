01 // PLATFORM STRATEGY

Khaotic Labs' go-to-market is a zero-budget, organic "build in public" strategy. The goal: become the most visible solo AI developer in the Midwest by documenting the work, sharing the lessons, and proving capability through shipped products — not claims.

1.1  Platform Stack

LinkedIn (Personal)
Primary thought leadership channel. Long-form posts about AI engineering, architecture decisions, and client case studies. Target: CTOs, VPs of Engineering, startup founders.

LinkedIn (Company)
Khaotic Labs company page. Reposts from personal + polished studio updates, service announcements, and project launches. Target: enterprise buyers.

X / Twitter
@KhaoticLabs. Real-time hot takes, quick tips, code snippets, and community engagement. Fastest growth channel for developer audiences. Target: developer community, AI Twitter.

1.2  Buffer Setup (Free Tier)

Connect 3 channels: LinkedIn Personal + LinkedIn Company + X. Schedule up to 10 posts per channel. Batch content creation weekly using Claude Pro or Gemini Advanced. Schedule on Sunday for the full week ahead.

03 // CONTENT CALENDAR — WEEK 1 TEMPLATE

This is a reusable weekly template. Rotate topics within each pillar slot each week. All posts drafted with Claude Pro or Gemini Advanced, edited for voice, scheduled in Buffer.

MONDAY — BUILD IN PUBLIC (PILLAR #1)

  POST TYPE: Project update or sprint recap

"Shipped sprint 3 of [project] today. Here's what the architecture looks like now and the one thing I would do differently if starting over. [diagram or code screenshot]"

Platform: LinkedIn Personal (primary) + X thread (expanded version)

TUESDAY — TEACH THE STACK (PILLAR #2)

  POST TYPE: Tool breakdown or technical explainer

"Most devs are sleeping on [tool]. Here's how I use it at Khaotic Labs to [solve problem]. [3-5 bullet breakdown + code snippet]"

Platform: X (primary, thread format) + LinkedIn short-form

WEDNESDAY — BUILD IN PUBLIC (PILLAR #1)

  POST TYPE: Demo, GIF, or screenshot drop

"Here's a quick demo of [feature] running in production. Built this in [timeframe] using [Claude/Gemini/Go/etc.]. The terminal output alone is clean as hell. [attach GIF or screenshot]"

Platform: X (primary) + LinkedIn Company (repost)

THURSDAY — TEACH THE STACK (PILLAR #2)

  POST TYPE: Comparison, myth-bust, or JPE explainer

"A lot of people ask me [question]. Here's the real answer in plain English: [3-paragraph breakdown]. No jargon, no gatekeeping."

Platform: LinkedIn Personal (primary, long-form)

FRIDAY — BUSINESS INTEL (PILLAR #3)

  POST TYPE: Solo founder lesson, pricing, or process post

"Sent my first invoice as Khaotic Labs LLC this week. Here's exactly how I structured the fixed-fee scope to prevent scope creep and protect my time. Template at the bottom."

Platform: LinkedIn Personal (primary) + X short version

WEEKEND — STUDIO SIGNALS (PILLAR #4, AS NEEDED)

  POST TYPE: Announcement, launch, or milestone

Use weekends for studio-level posts: project launches, service updates, or reflecting on the week's builds. These are lower-frequency, higher-polish posts.

Platform: All three channels simultaneously

04 // CONTENT GENERATION WORKFLOW

Use this exact workflow every Sunday to batch the entire week's content in under 2 hours:

Step 1: Open Claude Pro. Drop in the week's actual work context (what you built, what broke, what you learned).

Step 2: Prompt: "Draft 5 social media posts using the Khaotic Labs content calendar template below. Use a direct, technical, AAVE-inflected voice. No corporate language. Each post should clearly serve one of the four content pillars." [attach pillar definitions].

Step 3: Review, edit for voice, add real screenshots or code snippets.

Step 4: Open Buffer. Schedule each post to its platform slot at optimal times (see below).

Step 5: Set LinkedIn Company page to auto-repost from personal feed where relevant.

4.1  Optimal Posting Times (EST)
LinkedIn
Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am or 5–6pm

X (Twitter)
Weekdays, 9–11am or 3–5pm

Weekends
Studio Signals only — Saturday 10am if needed

6.2  Engagement Rules

Comment on 3-5 posts per day in your target audience (CTOs, AI engineers, startup founders). Provide genuine value in comments — not just "Great post!"

Respond to every comment on your own posts within 24 hours.

DM new followers who match ideal client profile (ICP) with a genuine, non-pitchy opener. Lead with curiosity, not sales.

Track ICP engagements in a simple Turso-backed spreadsheet or Notion table. Add notes after every interaction.
